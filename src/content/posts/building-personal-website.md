This is a journey of my professional experience. It started in 2016. I graduated in February, and I had a gap of about 6 months to help my grandma. Then I got my first job at [Radya Labs](https://radyalabs.com/id) with help from my college friend, his name is [Albilaga](https://github.com/albilaga).

During the first 6 months there, I was working as an Android developer, and in the first 2 months I almost gave up due to deadline pressure, lack of coding experience during college, and UI challenges. Thank god, I survived. 

After busting my a** off, with countless sleepless nights to catch up with the knowledge and deadlines, I successfully delivered my first ever production mobile application to support BKKBN agents on field to submit their work. I was thrilled. I experienced traveling to Padang City to meet with BKKBN agents, talking with them, countless trips between Bandung and Jakarta to meet with the clients to ensure smooth delivery. A Priceless experience.

Long story short, the company shifted me to a backend developer using .NET. From there, I began to really love it. I love the complexity of working with the backend. You know what? Not only frontend needs design, backend does too! With proper design, it becomes much easier to adapt to changes. I learned it the hard way. Along the way, I began to fear HTML, CSS, CSS animations, YAML design, GRID/XAML, and so on.

***

In the era of AI, mastering only one thing, in my opinion might slowly make you outdated. So, I began to face my fear and start learning CSS. Thank God we have Tailwind CSS, whoever made it, you guys are superb. And of course, with the help of AI, I learned those required things more fast and smoothly.

And here we are, if you, the reader, are reading this post, it means I have successfully created my personal website and outplayed my fear :))
